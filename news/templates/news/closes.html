How this is counted

  • In: stories in the Fundraising topic whose headline says a fund closed, raised or secured money and states a size.
  • Out: targets and rumours, such as "eyes" or "targets", until a close is reported.
  • One fund, one count: reports of the same event are grouped. When reports give different sizes, the lower one is used.
  • Sizes: read from headlines by a simple rule, so some may be wrong. Euros and pounds are converted to dollars at rough fixed rates.
